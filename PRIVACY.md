# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Tender for Frigate ("Tender", "the app") is a client for Frigate NVR, self-hosted camera
software. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

## Summary

Tender does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer has no server and receives nothing
from the app.

## What the app stores on your device

- The Frigate servers you add: name, address and (optional) username. Passwords are stored in
  the iOS Keychain.
- App settings, such as your selected server and display preferences.

This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

## Network connections

The app only connects to the Frigate servers that you add yourself, to show live video,
recordings, events and snapshots. These connections go directly from your device to your own
server; they do not pass through any server of the developer.

When you look for servers on your local network, the app probes addresses on that network to
find Frigate. This information is not sent anywhere.

## Photos

When you save a snapshot or video clip, the app asks permission to add it to your photo
library. It never reads your photo library.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
